## Notes & Guidance

Participants choose a path through the Agent Observability and Optimization
workshop and identify the learning outcomes that matter to them. Fundamentals
is the required readiness track, not a participant skill level: its goal is a
known-good environment and verified prompt-agent and hosted-agent baselines.

**Participant lab:** [Fundamentals 00 - Overview](https://github.com/microsoft-foundry/agent-optimization-workshop/blob/code-club-2026/labs/fundamentals/00-overview.md)

### Key Points

- Frame the full Agent DevOps lifecycle as Plan, Build, Evaluate, Deploy,
  Monitor, and Optimize, with Protect and red-team practices cross-cutting.
- Explain that the techniques apply to prompt agents, hosted agents, and other
  generative AI workloads.
- Route beginners through Fundamentals and the first Core lab, experienced
  participants through Fundamentals verification and Core Labs, and advanced
  participants to choose-your-own-adventure work only after the readiness gate.
- Reinforce that success is progress plus shared learning: teams should explain
  what they tried, what changed, what failed, and what they would do next.
- Establish governance and security as production requirements, not optional
  follow-up work.

### Coaching Questions

- What evidence would the organization need before allowing this workload into
  production?
- Which parts of the workflow should be automated through DevOps?
- When is a prompt agent sufficient, and when is a hosted agent justified?
- What does success look like for this session beyond completing every lab?

### Success Criteria

Each team has confirmed its learning goals and route, understands that
Fundamentals is required before Core or stretch work, and can describe how
observability and evaluation support a production AI governance model.

### Common Pitfalls

- Teams attempting every optional path without leaving time for evaluation.
- Participants treating a successful model response as sufficient production
  evidence.

### Time Management

**Expected Duration:** 10 minutes

Use this time to choose one provisioning path and confirm the team's learning
goal. Move into provisioning once participants can name the scenario, the Agent
DevOps loop, and their selected path.
